Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YolkPay Inc. (“YolkPay,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and discloses personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or submit an initial merchant inquiry.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide, including your name, business name, email address, phone number, website, industry, expected processing information, and the contents of messages you send us. We may also receive basic technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, referring pages, and website activity when analytics or security services are enabled.

How we use information

  • Respond to questions and merchant inquiries;
  • Assess which YolkPay products may suit your business;
  • Operate, secure, and improve this website;
  • Communicate about applications, products, and services;
  • Meet legal, regulatory, risk, and compliance obligations.

Merchant onboarding

A merchant application may include business registration, ownership, identity, banking, transaction, and compliance information, together with supporting documents such as articles of incorporation, government-issued identification, and a void cheque or bank letter. This information is collected through YolkPay’s dedicated merchant registration process, stored in access-controlled systems, and used to assess, verify, underwrite, and administer merchant services. Do not provide merchant onboarding documents through the general contact form.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers, payment partners, professional advisers, regulators, law enforcement, or other parties when reasonably required to provide services, review an application, comply with law, protect rights, or complete a business transaction. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and applicable legal, regulatory, dispute, and recordkeeping requirements. Merchant application records and documents are stored separately from the public website and are available only through authenticated administrative access. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to access or correct your personal information, or withdraw consent where applicable, subject to legal and contractual restrictions. You may also opt out of marketing communications.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact YolkPay Inc. at 21-401 Alden Rd, Markham, ON, Canada, or email info@yolkpay.com.
